How do you decide which actions need approval, and would you build or buy the controls?

Fit criteriaAgents act in external systems that change money, accounts or customer data, with public evidence and an identifiable control owner. Max two contacts per company.

Success path
  1. 1Substantive reply
  2. 2Discovery call
  3. 3Technical follow-up

How I approached them
  1. Select by observable actionTier 1 companies, then lookalikes, then smaller builders with public money-moving agents.
  2. Reach the closest ownerFounder first, then CTO or AI/Product leads; verify roles and existing threads.
  3. Personalize the first touchOne specific product action, not a generic AI pitch.
  4. Change the ask over timeProduct intro → advice request → concrete money-flow scenario.
  5. Follow up with permissionThank-you and site link after accepting; each later follow-up approved individually.
